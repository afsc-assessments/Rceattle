Available food, predation mortality (M2), and consumed biomass (MSVPA).

Available food is suitability-weighted prey biomass plus "other food": prey numbers (thousands of fish) x weight (kg) = mt, and other_food is mt. Under msmMode = 2 (Type III) the prey term is squared, so the denominator is not a biomass there. M2 divides each predator's consumption (avgN x ration) by that denominator, so a LARGER available-food denominator gives LESS predation mortality. The dB0 (F = 0) and dBF (F = Ftarget) arrays repeat the calculation for the dynamic reference points. Prey biomass and the predator scalars are each computed once outside the deepest loop.

MSVPA/empirical suitability, from stomach content and prey biomass.

For predators with suitMode = 0. stom_div_bio is the observed diet proportion divided by prey biomass (numbers in thousands x weight in kg = mt; the prey term is squared under msmMode = 2, Type III). That ratio is normalized over the modeled prey and "other food", averaged over suit_styr..suit_endyr, and held constant across all years.

A prey age whose average numbers approach zero with a positive diet proportion sends stom_div_bio, and so the prey term, to infinity: that one cell takes the whole normalized share, "other food" vanishes, available food collapses with it, so M2 diverges. The other-food term is floored at a fixed fraction of the prey term, bounding suit_other below at floor/(1 + floor), leaving sum(suitability) + suit_other = 1 exact. .warn_suitability_floor() reports when it binds.

Parametric suitability (Gamma, Lognormal, Normal) from size ratios.

Evaluates the predator-to-prey size ratio against a density scaled to a peak of 1. suitMode sets both the density and the size metric:

  • Gamma: suitMode 1 (length-based) or 2 (weight-based)
  • Lognormal: suitMode 3 (length-based) or 4 (weight-based)
  • Normal: suitMode 5 (length-based) or 6 (weight-based) The length-based modes 1, 3 and 5 are NOT YET AVAILABLE: they need an active growth model, and data_check() blocks them until that validation is added.

Transforms predator-prey preference parameters into vulnerability.

Multinomial logistic transform: each predator's prey preferences and its vulnerability to "other food" sum to 1. Parametric suitability only (suitMode > 0). Adopted from Trijoulet et al. 2020 (https://github.com/vtrijoulet/Multisp_model_JAE/blob/master/MS_SSM.cpp).
