Annual ration of a predator at age, in kg per predator per year.

Updates consumption_at_age in place, from the bioenergetics equations (Ceq 1-3) or from input consumption (Ceq 4).

Temperature-dependent function of consumption (fT) for bioenergetics.

Updates the fT matrix in place. Supports multiple bioenergetics models: 1 = Exponential function (Stewart et al. 1983) 2 = Warm-water species temperature dependence (Kitchell et al. 1977) 3 = Cool/cold-water species temperature dependence (Thornton and Lessem 1979) 4 = Constant (fT = 1.0)

Parameters
fTTemperature functions [nspp, nyrs], updated.
nsppNumber of species.
nyrsYears, hindcast plus projection.
CeqConsumption equation per species.
CindexEnvironmental index driving each species.
QcQ coefficient in equations 1-2, a temperature in equation 3.
TcmThermal max.
TcoThermal optimum.
TclThermal limit for Thornton/Lessem.
CK1Ascending limit for Thornton/Lessem.
CK4Descending limit for Thornton/Lessem.
env_indexEnvironmental predictors, e.g. bottom temperature [nyrs, n_indices].
